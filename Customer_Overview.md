# FreshGrupo Mobile Application - Customer Overview

## Welcome to FreshGrupo!

**FreshGrupo** is your convenient mobile solution for fresh produce and grocery delivery right to your doorstep. We bring farm-fresh vegetables, juicy fruits, and essential groceries directly to you through our easy-to-use subscription service.

## What We Offer

### 🥕 Fresh Categories Available
- **Vegetables**: Fresh, seasonal vegetables delivered at peak ripeness (Weekly, Bi-Weekly)
- **Fruits**: Juicy, hand-picked fruits for maximum nutrition and taste (Weekly, Bi-Weekly)
- **Groceries**: Essential pantry items and daily necessities (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly)

### 📦 Flexible Subscription Plans
Choose the plan that fits your lifestyle:

| Plan | Frequency | Price | Best For |
|------|-----------|-------|----------|
| **Weekly** | Every week | ₹2,500 | Regular fresh produce needs |
| **Bi-Weekly** | Every 2 weeks | ₹4,500 | Moderate usage households |

## How It Works

### 1. **Get Started**
- Download the FreshGrupo app from App Store or Google Play
- Create your account or sign in
- You're ready to start shopping!

### 2. **Choose Your Category**
- Browse our three main categories: Vegetables, Fruits, or Groceries
- Each category contains carefully curated, high-quality items
- Select based on your current needs or preferences

### 3. **Select Your Pack**
- Choose from our flexible subscription plans
- See exactly what's included in each pack
- Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
- Customize your delivery frequency

### 4. **Add to Cart & Checkout**
- Add selected packs to your shopping cart
- Review your order details and pricing
- Select delivery address and preferred time
- Complete payment securely

### 5. **Delivery Made Easy**
- Track your order status in real-time
- Receive notifications for order updates
- Get fresh items delivered at your convenience
- Rate your delivery experience
